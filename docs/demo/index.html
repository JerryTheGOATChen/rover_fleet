Avg known is the share of the house the base knew, averaged over the whole mission: higher means the operator learns the layout sooner.

Learned is a policy trained with multi-agent PPO on thousands of generated houses it never repeats; every 3 s each robot scores its options (frontiers, teammates, the base, staying put as a relay) and Nav2-style path following does the driving. Return-to-base explores greedily and heads home after 60 s out of contact (the best value on separate tuning houses). Greedy always takes the nearest large frontier and only comes home when nothing is left.

Every replay uses the same simulator seed, so differences come from the strategy alone. The Gazebo house is the 16 × 12 m world the ROS stack runs in; the generated house was never seen in training.
